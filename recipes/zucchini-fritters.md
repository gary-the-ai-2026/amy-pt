# Zucchini Fritters

**Source:** Boob to Food (cookbook, page 335)
**Makes:** 12
**Added:** 2026-07-26

## Ingredients

| # | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1 kg grated zucchini |
| 2 | 2 tsp sea salt |
| 3 | 3 large eggs |
| 4 | 4 Tbsp finely chopped dill or chives |
| 5 | 5 Tbsp crumbled feta cheese |
| 6 | 1 garlic clove, minced |
| 7 | 1 lemon, zested and cut into wedges (to serve) |
| 8 | ½ cup cassava flour (tapioca or chickpea flour also work) |
| 9 | 1 tsp baking powder |
| | 2 Tbsp ghee or extra virgin olive oil (for frying) |

## Method

1. Grate zucchini, transfer to fine sieve, toss with salt, set over bowl. Sit 10 min until excess moisture releases.
2. Grab handfuls of zucchini and squeeze/wring out ALL excess liquid. Then place into paper towel and scrunch to get even more liquid out — the drier the better. There will be a lot of liquid.
3. Add eggs to large bowl and whisk. Add zucchini, dill/chives, feta, garlic, lemon zest, flour, and baking powder. Stir until combined.
4. Heat large frying pan over medium heat, add 2 Tbsp ghee or olive oil. Scoop ¼ cup of mixture and place into hot pan. Cook 2–3 min each side until golden brown. Transfer to paper towel-lined plate. Repeat with remaining mixture, adding more ghee/oil if needed.
5. Serve with lemon wedges squeezed over.

## Notes

- Keeps in fridge up to 3 days, or freeze up to 3 months.
- Freezer option: omit feta cheese.
- Serving suggestion: delicious with a fried egg on top.
- Pairs well with poached salmon, sardines, or mackerel.
- Lunchbox friendly. Egg-free option available.

## Macros (estimated, per fritter)

| Per fritter (12/batch) |
|---|
| **Protein** | ~4g |
| **Fibre** | ~1g |
| **Calories** | ~85 |

*Most protein from eggs and feta. Zucchini adds bulk and fibre. Cassava flour keeps it gluten-free.*
