# 4-Ingredient Chocolate Mousse

**Source:** its_scarlett_lee (Instagram)
**Added:** 2026-07-26

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Greek yoghurt | 1 cup |
| Cacao powder | 2 Tbsp |
| Honey or maple syrup | 2 Tbsp (or more to taste) |
| Gelatin | 1 Tbsp mixed with ½ cup boiling water |

## Method

1. Mix gelatin with boiling water and stir well to dissolve.
2. Add all ingredients to a blender or food processor. Blend until smooth.
3. Pour into small glass jars. Pop in fridge until set.

## Variations

- Add vanilla powder.
- Add a couple drops of orange or peppermint essential oil.
- Stir through some berries.
- Adjust sweetness to your liking.

## Macros (estimated, full batch)

| Full batch (~2–3 serves) | Per serve (⅓ batch) |
|---|---|
| **Protein** | ~24g | ~8g |
| **Calories** | ~340 | ~113 |

*Greek yoghurt is the protein base (~20g per cup). Gelatin adds another ~4g. Cacao adds fibre. Honey/maple for sweetness. Protein-rich, no whey needed.*
