# Sardine & Yoghurt Dip

**Source:** boobtofood (Instagram)
**Added:** 2026-07-26

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Notes |
|---|---|
| Sardines | 1 tin (~120g), spicy or plain. Stock Merchant recommended. |
| Greek yoghurt | ~2–3 Tbsp |
| Lemon juice | To taste |
| Capers | ~1 tsp |
| Red onion | Finely diced, ~1 Tbsp |
| Spring onion | Sliced |
| Dill | If you have it (she didn't) |

## Method

1. Drain sardines. Mash with a fork in a bowl.
2. Add Greek yoghurt, lemon juice, capers, red onion, and spring onion.
3. Mix well. Serve as a spread on toast, crackers, or as a dip with cucumber slices.

Sardines available at Harris Farm Markets, partandparcel.store. Discount code: BOOBTOFOOD on Stock Merchant website.
