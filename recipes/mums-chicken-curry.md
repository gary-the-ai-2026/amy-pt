# Mum's Chicken Curry

**Source:** Cookbook (recipe photo, 26/7/26)
**Serves:** 4

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Ghee or unsalted butter | 2 Tbsp |
| Rapeseed oil | 1 Tbsp |
| Cumin seeds | 1 tsp |
| Cinnamon sticks | 2 × 4cm |
| Onions (large, finely chopped) | 2 |
| Ginger (peeled and grated) | 6cm |
| Garlic (crushed) | 6 cloves |
| Fresh green chillies (or 1 tsp chilli powder) | 2 |
| Salt | To taste |
| Tomato passata | 200g |
| Tomato purée | 2 Tbsp |
| Ground cumin | 1½ tsp |
| Ground turmeric | ½ tsp |
| Whole-milk yoghurt (+ extra to serve) | 3 Tbsp |
| Skinless chicken legs (or 800g skinless boneless thighs) | 1.2kg |
| Ground almonds | 3 Tbsp |
| Garam masala | 1 tsp |

## Method

1. Put ghee and oil into a wide-bottomed, lidded frying pan on medium heat. When hot, add cumin seeds and cinnamon sticks. Infuse for 1 minute.
2. Add onions. Cook 10–12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until golden brown.
3. Meanwhile, put ginger, garlic, and green chillies into a pestle and mortar with a pinch of salt. Bash to a coarse paste.
4. Add paste to the pan. Cook gently for 2 minutes.
5. Pour in passata and stir. Cook for a few minutes until it resembles a thick paste.
6. Add tomato purée, ground cumin, turmeric, and ½ tsp salt (or to taste).
7. Whisk the yoghurt and add it slowly to the curry. Cook through until it starts to bubble.
8. Add chicken. Pop the lid on and continue to cook on gentle heat for ~30 minutes.
9. Add ground almonds and garam masala. Cook another 5 minutes.
10. Serve with chapatis, naan, or rice. Offer extra yoghurt at the table.

## Macros (estimated, per serve — curry only, no rice)

| | Per serve (based on 800g thighs) |
|---|---|
| **Protein** | ~43g |
| **Fibre** | ~3g |
| **Calories** | ~480 |

*Chicken thighs carry most of the protein. Passata, onions, and spices add fibre. Ground almonds add body and ~2g protein per serve. Serve with rice (~200 kcal per cup) or chapati (~100 kcal each).*
