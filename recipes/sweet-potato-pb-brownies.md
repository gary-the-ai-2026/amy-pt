# Sweet Potato & Peanut Butter Swirl Brownies

**Source:** reneelynch_naturopath (Instagram, first shared 2013)
**Added:** 2026-07-26

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Sweet potato | 1 medium (2–3 cups peeled and grated) |
| Eggs | 2 |
| Coconut oil (melted) or olive oil | ½ cup |
| Maple syrup | ⅓ cup |
| Vanilla extract | 1 tsp |
| Cacao powder | ½ cup |
| Baking powder | 1 tsp |
| Baking soda | 1 tsp |
| Coconut flour | 2.5 Tbsp |
| Chocolate chips or chopped chocolate | Loaded ½ cup |
| Smooth peanut butter | ¼ cup |

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 180°C.
2. Mix wet ingredients (excluding peanut butter). Add baking powder, baking soda, and chopped chocolate. Mix in coconut flour.
3. Pour brownie mix into lined square baking tray. Drizzle with peanut butter and use a knife or skewer to swirl it through.
4. Bake for 30 minutes. Check and cook 5 minutes longer if needed — brownies should look slightly cracked on top.

## Options

- Add a scoop of collagen powder to the mix.
- Add frozen raspberries and skip PB altogether for lunchbox-friendly.
