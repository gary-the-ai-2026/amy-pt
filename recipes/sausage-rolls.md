# Sausage Rolls

**Source:** Boob to Food cookbook (page 344)
**Makes:** 18
**Tags:** Lunchbox Friendly, Freezable, Egg-free option, Gluten-free option

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Pork mince | 350g |
| Lamb mince | 350g |
| Garlic cloves, minced | 4 |
| Zucchini, grated (moisture squeezed out) | 1 cup |
| Carrot, grated | 1 cup |
| Ground coriander | 2–3 tsp |
| Sea salt and black pepper | Pinch |
| Free-range egg, whisked | 1 large |
| Butter puff pastry (partially thawed) | 3 sheets (500g) |
| Sesame or poppy seeds (garnish) | Sprinkle |

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 200°C. Line two trays with baking paper.
2. In a large bowl, combine pork and lamb mince, garlic, zucchini, carrot, coriander, salt, pepper, and half the whisked egg. Mix well with hands to combine. Set aside.
3. Slice each pastry sheet into thirds. Divide filling into 9 equal parts, form into long log shapes, and place in the middle of each pastry strip on the baking paper.
4. Gently lift and fold each side of pastry over the filling — they should overlap slightly. Turn the whole log over seam-side down, slice into 6 pieces, and place onto prepared baking trays.
5. Brush tops with remaining whisked egg. Sprinkle with sesame or poppy seeds and a pinch of flaky sea salt.
6. Bake for 15 minutes at 200°C, then reduce to 180°C fan-forced and bake 10–15 minutes more, until pastry is golden and sausage rolls are cooked through.
7. Transfer to a wire rack to keep the base crisp while cooling slightly before serving.

## Options

- **Egg-free:** Omit egg wash — brushing with milk yields a similar effect.
- **Gluten-free:** Substitute butter puff pastry with gluten-free puff pastry.
