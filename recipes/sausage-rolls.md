# Sausage Rolls

**Source:** Boob to Food (cookbook, page 344)
**Makes:** 18
**Added:** 2026-07-26

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Pork mince | 350g |
| Lamb mince | 350g |
| Garlic cloves, minced | 4 |
| Grated zucchini (excess moisture squeezed out) | 1 cup |
| Grated carrot | 1 cup |
| Ground coriander | 2–3 tsp |
| Sea salt and black pepper | Pinch |
| Large free-range egg, whisked | 1 |
| Butter puff pastry (partially thawed) | 3 sheets (500g) |
| Sesame or poppy seeds | For garnish |

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 200°C. Line two trays with baking paper.
2. In a large bowl, add pork and lamb mince, garlic, zucchini, carrot, coriander, salt and pepper, and ½ of the whisked egg (keep rest for egg wash). Mix well with hands to thoroughly combine.
3. Slice each pastry sheet into thirds. Divide mixture into 9 equal parts, forming long log shapes, placing in the middle of each pastry strip.
4. Gently lift and fold each side of pastry over filling — they should overlap slightly. Press together. Place seam side down. Slice each log into 6 pieces and place onto prepared trays.
5. Brush tops with remaining whisked egg. Sprinkle with sesame or poppy seeds and a pinch of flaky sea salt.
6. Bake 15–20 min, then reduce oven to 180°C fan-forced and bake extra 10–15 min, until pastry is golden and sausage rolls are cooked through.
7. Transfer to wire rack to maintain crisp base while cooling slightly before serving.

## Notes

- Freeze well for quick lunches. Serve warm or cold.
- Serve with homemade Tomato Sauce (page 282).
- Egg-free option: omit egg wash — brushing with milk yields similar effect.
- Gluten-free option: substitute butter puff pastry with GF puff pastry.
- Lunchbox friendly.
