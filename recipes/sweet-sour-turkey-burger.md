# Sweet & Sour Turkey Burger

**Source:** Amy's cookbook | **Base serves:** 6 | **Amy's batch:** 9 patties

## Ingredients — Turkey Patties

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Lean turkey mince | 500g |
| Whole egg mayonnaise | 1 Tbsp |
| Garlic clove (minced) | 1 |
| Red onion (finely diced) | ¼ cup (⅛ of whole) |
| Bird's eye chillies (finely diced) | 2 |
| Fresh parsley (finely chopped) | 1 Tbsp |
| Salt | ½ tsp |
| Pepper | Generous crack |
| Cayenne pepper | ¼ tsp |
| Ground cumin | ¼ tsp |
| Chilli powder | ¼ tsp |
| Gluten free bread crumbs | ¼ cup |
| Olive oil (for pan-frying) | Drizzle |

## Method

1. Add all turkey patty ingredients (except olive oil) to a large bowl and mix well.
2. Shape into patties (base recipe: 6; Amy typically gets 9 smaller patties).
3. Pan-fry in olive oil until cooked through.

## Amy's Notes

- *"Take the extra time to dice these as fine as possible — will help make the texture smoother"* (referring to red onion and bird's eye chillies)
- Always ask how many patties she made from the batch — she varies between 6 and 9.
