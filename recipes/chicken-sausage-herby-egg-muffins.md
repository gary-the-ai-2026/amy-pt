# Chicken Sausage & Herby Egg Muffins

**Source:** @reneelynch_naturopath (Instagram) | **Makes:** 12 muffins
**Per muffin:** ~446 cal · 38g P · 39g C · 15g F

## Ingredients

### Herby Egg Base
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Eggs | 12 |
| Milk | ½ cup |
| Fresh dill | ½ bunch, chopped |
| Spring onions | 3, sliced |
| Lemon zest | ½ lemon |
| Salt | To taste |

### Assembly
| Layer | Per muffin |
|---|---|
| English muffins | 12 (she uses @this.is.us.au) |
| Chicken patties | 12 (~87g each, from 1kg chicken mince) |
| Cheese slices | 12 |

## Method

### Egg Base
1. Preheat oven to 180°C
2. Whisk all egg ingredients together
3. Line a baking tray with parchment paper, pour mixture in
4. Bake 15 mins, check — blanket with foil for 5 more mins if needed, until set
5. Cool, then slice into 12 equal squares

### Assembly
1. Cook chicken patties (recipe linked separately on her page)
2. Stack each muffin: English muffin → egg square → chicken patty → cheese slice
3. Wrap individually in parchment paper
4. Refrigerate or freeze

### Reheating
- From fridge: bake 10 mins at 180°C
- From freezer: thaw first, then same
