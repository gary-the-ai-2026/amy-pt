# Jammy Eggs in Chilli Oil

**Source:** jordiepieface + partandparcel.store (Instagram)
**Added:** 2026-07-26

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Notes |
|---|---|
| Eggs | As many as you want |
| Tamari (or soy sauce) | Generous drizzle |
| Toasted sesame oil | Drizzle |
| Chilli oil | To taste |
| Spring onions or fresh parsley | Chopped, for garnish |
| **To serve** | |
| Steamed rice | Warm |
| Chicken bone broth concentrate | Optional — add to rice while cooking (Urban Forager recommended) |

## Method

1. Boil eggs for ~6.5 minutes (jammy yolks). Adjust to 7 min for firmer.
2. Transfer to cold water bath for ~5 minutes, then peel.
3. Add peeled eggs to a container. Drizzle over tamari, toasted sesame oil, and chilli oil.
4. Top with chopped spring onions or fresh parsley.
5. Serve warm over rice. Keeps for days in the fridge.
6. Also good on toast with avocado + rocket, or as a standalone snack.

## Macros (estimated, per egg)

| Per egg (with oils) | Per serve (2 eggs + 1 cup rice) |
|---|---|
| **Protein** | ~6g | ~20g |
| **Calories** | ~110 | ~400 |

*The oils add ~30 kcal per egg. Tamari adds sodium but negligible calories. If using bone broth concentrate in the rice, add ~2g protein and ~15 kcal per serve.*
